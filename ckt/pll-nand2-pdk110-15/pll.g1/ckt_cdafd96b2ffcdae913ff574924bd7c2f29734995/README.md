# pll_nand2_pdk110_15

Netlist of the subcircuit `pll_nand2_pdk110_15` of `pll`, turned into a TOA circuit with toa-virtuoso.

- `15_pll_nand2.scs`: the Spectre netlist, with its drawing in RNL 0.4 records.
- `pll_semantics.rnl`: the part of an RNL package of the source that the drawing uses.
- Subcircuits: none.
- The models of the devices of the process `tsmc180` are not included.
